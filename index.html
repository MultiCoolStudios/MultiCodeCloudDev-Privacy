Privacy Policy

MC Cloud Dev (Android) · by MultiCool Studios · Last updated: 24 October 2026

Account data

Online features are powered by MultiCode Backend (sign-in and cloud storage). Your email address and an encrypted session token are used for authentication. Your password is never stored on your phone. The session token is stored on your device using Android Keystore encryption.

Project data

Online projects (text files only) are stored on MultiCode Backend and linked to your account. Only you can read them. Offline projects stay on your device as ordinary folders and are never uploaded.

AI assistant (optional)

The assistant is off by default. If you add your own Google Gemini API key and consent, the messages you send, and the open file if you choose to include it, are sent to Google's Gemini API. Nothing is sent until you press Send. Your key is stored encrypted on your device.

GitHub import

When you ask to import a repository, the app downloads a ZIP of a public repository from GitHub. No GitHub account data is collected.

Crash log

If the app crashes, a report is written to a file on your device only. It is shared only if you choose to share it.

Ads, analytics and tracking

The app contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we do not sell any data.

Permissions

Internet access is used for Online mode, GitHub import and the optional AI assistant. Storage access is used only for Offline mode, so you can keep projects in folders on your phone.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings › Danger Zone to clear all your cloud data or to delete your account. You can also email us and we will delete it for you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
